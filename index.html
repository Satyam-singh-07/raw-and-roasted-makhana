About Poppinuts

Poppinuts is a premium healthy snacking brand made from air-popped water lily seeds, also known globally as lotus seeds or makhana. Celebrated for their high protein content and light texture, our seeds are a true superfood.

As a flagship brand of Shree Lotus Exports Pvt. Ltd., we are committed to superfood nutrition, purity, and premium quality. Our seeds are carefully sourced, air-popped without oil, and rigorously quality-checked.
